Director

Supported by: Keio University, Keio Media Design Working with Hong Kong performer Siufong Yeung and Japanese wheelchair dancer Kenta Kambara, we recently held a workshop, exploring how artists of different abilities engage with motion capture and avatar remapping in dance. Rather than framing technology as something that “empowers,” we looked at how each performer negotiates, adapts, and reimagines their practice when mediated by digital avatars. The sessions opened up fresh ways of thinking about inclusive, technology-supported performance, and highlighted the creative potential of movement beyond conventional forms....

Exhibition: An AI-Empowered Personality Testing Game 'Relato'

🔗 Youtube We seldom get opportunities to share our deeper thoughts and emotions with each other because societal norms discourage these informal practices, favoring formal discourse over emotional expression. Most importantly, without meaningful communication with others, we seem to lose our ability to recognize our own emotional state. Relato is a GenAI game designed to restore our capacity for self-disclosure through interaction and relationship-building with other agentic individuals in the game world....

Artistic Interests

Beyond dance, I explore traditional and digital artistic expressions, including Guzheng, painting, and LEGO-based creative problem-solving. Running keeps me energized and sharp, often inspiring new perspectives in my research. These interdisciplinary interests shape my approach to performance studies, allowing me to investigate the intersection of technology, embodiment, and artistic collaboration.

Teaching Experience

I’m an ENFJ, passionate about helping people grow and thrive. Teaching brings me joy, and I love creating a supportive and inspiring learning environment. For three years, I worked as a choreography teacher, guiding students in movement composition and performance. Teaching has been an incredibly fulfilling journey, allowing me to nurture creativity and confidence in my students. （My beloved students）
